# Lung Cancer Treatment Guide

Welcome to your comprehensive guide for understanding lung cancer treatment options. This resource provides evidence-based information to help you navigate your cancer journey.

## Understanding Lung Cancer

Lung cancer affects over 230,000 Americans annually and is the leading cause of cancer-related deaths. Early detection and personalized treatment approaches have significantly improved outcomes in recent years.

### Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)
- **Prevalence**: 85% of all lung cancers
- **Subtypes**: 
  - Adenocarcinoma (40%): Often peripheral, less associated with smoking
  - Squamous cell carcinoma (25%): Central location, strongly linked to smoking
  - Large cell carcinoma (10%): Can occur anywhere in the lung
- **Growth pattern**: Generally slower growing, better surgical outcomes
- **5-year survival**: 25% overall, 60% when caught early (Stage I)

### Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC)
- **Prevalence**: 15% of lung cancers
- **Characteristics**: Rapidly growing, early metastasis
- **Smoking association**: 95% occur in current or former smokers
- **Staging**: Limited stage (30%) vs. Extensive stage (70%)
- **5-year survival**: 7% overall, 31% for limited stage

## Treatment Options by Stage

### Stage I-II NSCLC (30% of cases)
**Primary Treatment**: Surgery (preferred when feasible)
- **Lobectomy**: Standard of care for most patients
- **Segmentectomy**: For limited lung function or small tumors
- **Adjuvant chemotherapy**: For Stage IB-II (improves 5-year survival by 5-15%)
- **Stereotactic body radiation therapy (SBRT)**: For inoperable patients (90% local control)

**Expected Outcomes**:
- Stage IA: 92% 5-year survival
- Stage IB: 68% 5-year survival  
- Stage IIA: 60% 5-year survival
- Stage IIB: 53% 5-year survival

### Stage III NSCLC (25% of cases)
**Resectable (Stage IIIA)**:
- **Neoadjuvant chemotherapy** + surgery + adjuvant therapy
- **Concurrent chemoradiation** if unresectable
- **Median survival**: 28-36 months with multimodal therapy

### Stage IV NSCLC (55% of cases)
**Treatment selection based on molecular testing**:
- **EGFR mutations**: Osimertinib (median PFS 19 months)
- **ALK rearrangements**: Alectinib (median PFS 34 months)
- **PD-L1 ≥50%**: Pembrolizumab monotherapy (median survival 26 months)
