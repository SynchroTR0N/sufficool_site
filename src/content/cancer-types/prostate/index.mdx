# Prostate Cancer Treatment Guide

Welcome to your comprehensive guide for understanding prostate cancer treatment options. This resource is designed to help you navigate your cancer journey with evidence-based information and interactive tools.

Understanding your diagnosis and treatment options is the first step in your journey. This guide provides:

- **Treatment Options**: Detailed information about surgery, radiation, hormone therapy, and active surveillance
- **Patient Journey**: Step-by-step guidance through diagnosis, planning, treatment, and recovery
- **Decision Tools**: Interactive calculators and decision trees to help you evaluate your options
- **Questions Generator**: Prepare for your appointments with personalized question lists
