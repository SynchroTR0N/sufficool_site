# SBRT for Prostate Cancer

Welcome to your comprehensive guide about SBRT for treating prostate cancer. This page provides evidence-based information to help you understand this treatment option and make informed decisions about your care.

## Treatment Overview

<ProgressiveContent>
  <div data-level="essential">
    SBRT is a radiation approach for treating prostate cancer. This treatment delivers precisely focused radiation to cancer cells while minimizing exposure to surrounding healthy tissue.
    
    **Key Points:**
    - Advanced radiation therapy technique
    - Precisely targeted treatment delivery
    - Shorter treatment schedule than conventional radiation
    - Outpatient procedure with minimal recovery time
  </div>
  
  <div data-level="detailed">
    **How SBRT Works:**
    
    SBRT uses advanced imaging and computer guidance to deliver high-dose radiation with extreme precision. The treatment targets the prostate while sparing nearby organs like the bladder and rectum.
    
    **Treatment Process:**
    1. Pre-treatment imaging and planning
    2. Precise positioning and delivery
    3. Real-time monitoring during treatment
    4. Post-treatment care and follow-up
    
    **What to Expect:**
    - Typically 5 treatment sessions over 1-2 weeks
    - Each session lasts about 30-60 minutes
    - No anesthesia required
    - Able to return to normal activities quickly
  </div>
  
  <div data-level="technical">
    **Clinical Mechanism:**
    
    SBRT delivers ablative doses of radiation (typically 35-40 Gy in 5 fractions) using advanced techniques like intensity-modulated radiation therapy (IMRT) or volumetric modulated arc therapy (VMAT).
    
    **Treatment Parameters:**
    - Hypofractionated dosing schedule
    - Image-guided delivery verification
    - Real-time motion tracking
    - Advanced treatment planning algorithms
    
    **Research Background:**
    Multiple phase II and III trials have demonstrated the efficacy and safety of SBRT for localized prostate cancer, with excellent biochemical control rates and acceptable toxicity profiles.
  </div>
</ProgressiveContent>

## When to Consider SBRT

This treatment may be appropriate if:

- You have localized prostate cancer (T1-T2)
- Your PSA level and Gleason score meet criteria
- You prefer a shorter treatment schedule
- You want to minimize time away from work/activities

## Preparation and Next Steps

**Before Treatment:**
- Complete imaging studies (MRI, CT simulation)
- Discuss treatment goals and expectations
- Arrange schedule for treatment sessions
- Review potential side effects and management

**During Treatment:**
- Follow positioning instructions carefully
- Report any discomfort during sessions
- Maintain regular communication with care team
- Continue normal activities between treatments

**After Treatment:**
- Attend all follow-up appointments
- Monitor for any delayed side effects
- Continue PSA monitoring as scheduled
- Maintain healthy lifestyle habits
