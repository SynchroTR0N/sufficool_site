# Understanding Prostate Cancer Staging

Staging is how doctors describe the size and spread of your cancer. Understanding your stage helps you and your medical team choose the best treatment approach.

## Key Staging Systems

### TNM Staging
- **T (Tumor)**: Size and extent of the main tumor
- **N (Nodes)**: Whether cancer has spread to nearby lymph nodes  
- **M (Metastasis)**: Whether cancer has spread to other parts of the body

### Gleason Score
The Gleason score describes how aggressive your cancer cells appear under a microscope.

- **Score 6**: Low-grade cancer, grows slowly
- **Score 7**: Intermediate-grade cancer
- **Score 8-10**: High-grade cancer, more aggressive

## Risk Categories

Based on your staging information, cancers are typically grouped into risk categories:

- **Low Risk**: Early-stage, slow-growing cancers
- **Intermediate Risk**: Moderate risk of progression
- **High Risk**: More aggressive cancers requiring immediate treatment

## What This Means for You

Your staging information helps determine:
- Treatment urgency
- Available treatment options
- Expected outcomes
- Need for additional testing

Understanding your specific situation is crucial for making informed treatment decisions.
