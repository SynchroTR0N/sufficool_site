# How to Use This Site

This platform is designed to help you navigate your cancer journey with reliable, evidence-based information. Here's how to make the most of its features.

## Getting Started

### Choose Your Cancer Type
Begin by selecting your specific cancer type from the main navigation:
- **Prostate Cancer**: Comprehensive information about prostate cancer treatment
- **Lung Cancer**: Resources for lung cancer patients
- *Additional cancer types will be added over time*

### Understand the Organization
Content is organized into three main areas:
- **Cancer-Specific Information**: Detailed guides for each cancer type
- **Shared Resources**: Universal topics relevant to all cancer patients
- **About**: Information about the platform and Dr. Sufficool

## Navigation Features

### Patient Journey Approach
Follow your treatment journey step-by-step:
1. **Diagnosis**: Understanding your test results and what they mean
2. **Planning**: Exploring treatment options and making decisions
3. **Treatment**: What to expect during active treatment
4. **Recovery**: Managing side effects and healing
5. **Follow-up**: Long-term monitoring and survivorship

### Progressive Information Levels
Information is presented at different levels:
- **Essential**: Basic information everyone should know
- **Detailed**: More comprehensive explanations
- **Technical**: In-depth medical details for those who want them

### Interactive Tools
Look for interactive features throughout the site:
- **Decision Trees**: Step-by-step guidance for treatment choices
- **Risk Calculators**: Personalized risk assessments
- **Question Generators**: Prepare for medical appointments
- **Visual Aids**: Diagrams and animations to explain concepts

## Content Features

### Evidence-Based Information
All medical content is:
- Based on current clinical research
- Regularly updated with new findings
- Referenced with citations when appropriate
- Reviewed for accuracy and completeness

### Practical Focus
Information emphasizes practical applications:
- What to expect during procedures
- How to manage side effects
- Tips for communicating with your medical team
- Resources for additional support

## Making the Most of Your Visit

### Before Medical Appointments
- Review relevant sections for your situation
- Use question generators to prepare for discussions
- Note topics you want to discuss with your doctor
- Print or bookmark important information

### During Treatment
- Check side effect management guides
- Use timeline tools to track your progress
- Review what to expect at each treatment phase
- Access emotional support resources

### For Ongoing Reference
- Bookmark sections relevant to your situation
- Return to review information as needed
- Share appropriate sections with family members
- Use as a reference for long-term follow-up

## Important Reminders

### This Platform Is NOT a Substitute for Medical Care
- Always consult your healthcare team for personal medical decisions
- Don't delay seeking medical attention for urgent concerns
- Use this information to complement, not replace, professional medical advice
- Individual situations may differ from general information provided

### When to Contact Your Medical Team
Contact your healthcare providers for:
- Questions about your specific diagnosis or treatment
- Concerning symptoms or side effects
- Changes in your condition
- Personal medical decision-making

### Privacy and Security
- This platform doesn't collect personal medical information
- No account creation is required
- Your browsing activity isn't tracked for medical purposes
- Information is provided for educational purposes only

## Technical Features

### Mobile-Friendly Design
- Access information on any device
- Responsive design adapts to your screen size
- Key information remains easily readable

### Search Functionality
- Find specific topics quickly
- Search across all content areas
- Filter results by cancer type or topic

### Print-Friendly Content
- Important pages can be printed for offline reference
- Share with family members or caregivers
- Bring printed summaries to medical appointments

## Getting Help

### If You Need Technical Assistance
- Contact information is available in the site footer
- Report any broken links or technical issues
- Suggest improvements or additional content topics

### For Medical Questions
- Contact Dr. Sufficool's office for appointment-related questions
- Reach out to your healthcare team for urgent medical concerns
- Consider support groups for peer connections

## Continuous Improvement

This platform is regularly updated with:
- New research findings
- Additional interactive tools
- Expanded content areas
- User feedback improvements

Your feedback helps make this resource more valuable for all patients facing cancer.
