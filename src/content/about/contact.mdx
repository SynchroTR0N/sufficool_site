## Emergency Medical Situations

### When to Seek Immediate Care
**Call 911 or go to the emergency room if you experience:**
- Severe shortness of breath
- Chest pain
- Signs of serious infection (fever over 100.4°F during treatment)
- Severe bleeding
- Sudden severe pain
- Any other serious medical emergency

### Urgent but Non-Emergency Situations
**Contact Dr. Sufficool's office immediately for:**
- Concerning side effects during treatment
- Unexpected symptoms
- Questions about continuing treatment
- Medication concerns
