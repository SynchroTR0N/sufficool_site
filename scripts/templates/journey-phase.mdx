## Common Concerns and How to Address Them

**Emotional Concerns:**
- Anxiety about the unknown
- Fear about treatment outcomes
- Worry about impact on family

**How to Cope:**
- Talk with your care team about concerns
- Consider counseling or support groups
- Use relaxation and stress management techniques
- Stay connected with supportive family and friends

**Practical Concerns:**
- Work and financial impact
- Caregiving arrangements
- Transportation to appointments
- Managing household responsibilities

**How to Address:**
- Speak with social worker about resources
- Explore employer benefits and accommodations
- Organize support network early
- Consider community resources and assistance programs

**Medical Concerns:**
- Understanding treatment options
- Potential side effects
- Treatment effectiveness
- Long-term outcomes

**How to Get Clarity:**
- Prepare questions for your medical team
- Request written information
- Bring a support person to appointments
- Ask for clarification when needed
