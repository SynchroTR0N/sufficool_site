## Understanding Your Results

### What the Numbers Mean

**Survival Estimates:**
- These represent statistical averages based on similar patients
- Individual outcomes may vary significantly
- Results are probabilities, not certainties
- Consider confidence intervals provided

**Treatment Benefit:**
- Shows potential improvement with treatment
- Based on clinical trial data
- May not apply to all individual situations
- Discuss relevance with your medical team

### Important Limitations

<MedicalDisclaimer>
  **Calculator Limitations:**
  
  - Based on population averages, not individual guarantees
  - May not include all relevant factors
  - Results depend on accuracy of input information
  - Cannot predict individual outcomes with certainty
  - Should supplement, not replace, medical consultation
  
  **When Results May Not Apply:**
  - Rare cancer subtypes or presentations
  - Significant comorbidities not captured
  - Novel or experimental treatments
  - Recent changes in standard of care
</MedicalDisclaimer>
